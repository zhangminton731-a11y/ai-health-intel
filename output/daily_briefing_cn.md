Projected Effect of 2026 Federal Changes to the Childhood Immunization Schedule on Chronic Hepatitis B Virus Infections in the United States.
Background The United States recently proposed changes to the recommendations for childhood hepatitis B virus (HBV) vaccination. Objective To project the effect of vaccine policy changes, if fully implemented, on chronic HBV infections. Design Markov cohort state transition model of perinatal and childhood HBV infection. Data sources Published data on maternal HBV prevalence (0.4%) and hepatitis B surface antigen testing (99.5% sensitivity), childhood vaccine completion (universal birth-dose strategy: 91.1%; deferred strategy: 87.1%; shared decision-making strategy: 50.0%), and age-stratified risk for chronic HBV infection. Target population One-year birth cohort of U.S.-born infants ( n = 3.6 million). Time horizon 20 years. Intervention Universal birth-dose vaccination, deferred (2-month) vaccination, and vaccination through shared decision making.

Diagnosis and Treatment of Cardiovascular Conditions in Medicare Advantage In-Home Health Risk Assessments.
Background Medicare Advantage insurers conduct in-home health risk assessments (HRAs) to identify high-risk patients and facilitate appropriate care. Critics argue this practice increases risk-adjusted payments without improving quality. Comprehensive data on use of in-home HRAs and downstream treatment are lacking. Objective To examine trends in cardiovascular conditions diagnosed during in-home HRAs, characteristics associated with HRA-based diagnoses, and treatment rates after diagnoses. Design Cohort study. Setting Population-based. Participants Medicare Advantage beneficiaries. Measurements Using 100% Medicare claims, trends in the proportion of cardiovascular conditions diagnosed during in-home HRAs (vs. facility-based visits) were examined.

GUARD-LGG: a glioma upfront AI-driven radiotherapy response decipher for lower-grade glioma
Here we present GUARD-LGG, a machine learning model designed to predict early RT response in LGG before treatment. GUARD-LGG achieved AUCs of 0.946 (95% CI: 0.938–0.962), 0.929 (95% CI: 0.894–0.965), 0.882 (95% CI: 0.830–0.934), and 0.913 (95% CI: 0.878–0.949) in the training, internal validation, external, and prospective test sets, respectively.
