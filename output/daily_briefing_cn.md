A generalizable deep learning model for predicting opioid use disorder using language modeling for cross-EHR data harmonization
We developed OUD-REY (OUD Risk Evaluator at 1 Year) - a deep learning model that uses longitudinal electronic health record (EHR) data from the All of Us research program to predict risk of OUD at one year for patients with an opioid prescription. On internal validation, OUD-REY achieved an AUROC of 91.2% and AUPRC of 77.0%. We externally validated our model on a new dataset released by All of Us that was temporally separated from our development set and on a dataset containing ~6 million patients from medical centers of the Veterans Affairs (VA) nationwide over a span of 20 years. On external validation, our model had an AUROC and AUPRC of 86.4% and 23.1% (OUD prevalence of 2.9%), respectively, on our temporally separated All of Us validation set.

03 · 论文研究
Molecular predictors of minimal residual disease in newly diagnosed multiple myeloma treated with quadruplet therapy
Quadruplet therapy, combining anti-CD38 antibodies, immunomodulatory drugs, proteasome inhibitors and dexamethasone, is now the standard induction treatment for newly diagnosed multiple myeloma (NDMM). These MRD-associated transcriptomic features were independently validated in the CASSIOPEIA cohort. Whole-genome sequencing revealed both known (APOBEC mutagenesis, chromothripsis) and new genomic instability phenotypes validated in the MMRF cohort. A multimodal random forest model integrating genomic, transcriptomic and epigenomic features predicted MRD status after induction, with an area under the curve of 0.77, outperforming clinical and genomic models, while providing comparable discrimination and improved calibration relative to the transcriptomic and epigenomic models.
